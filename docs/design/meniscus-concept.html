Emberphase · Wear OS watch face proposal

Meniscus

A sealed glass core of luminous fluid that always knows which way is down. The time sits in the fluid and breaks at the waterline. The rim of the dial shows the next twelve hours of weather at the hours they will happen.

Drag the watch sideways to roll your wrist. Click the core to make the fluid slosh. All data is sample data.

Watch state

Battery at 20 percent or less: tilt, parallax, seconds and slosh are off.

Fluid colour

What has not been on a watch before

Gravity-true fluid core

The accelerometer drives the waterline, so the fluid stays level as you roll your wrist. Its level is the battery by default, or the time left in the day, or any pod's goal progress.

Waterline numerals

Digits glow on dark glass above the surface and turn dark against the lit fluid below it. The submerged half shifts slightly, like a straw breaking in a glass of water.

Chronocast ring

Each clock hour on the rim carries that hour's forecast: temperature as terrain depth, conditions as colour and texture, daylight and night as a horizon band. The hour cursor walks into the forecast.

Notification bubbles

Unread notifications rise as bubbles in the fluid, with a small count beside them. No bubbles means nothing is waiting.

Slosh

Tap the core and the fluid swings and settles over about three seconds. The watch companion supplies the tap; nothing runs in the background.

Charged conduits

Each pod docks to the core through a short conduit. When the pod reaches a real goal, its conduit lights up, as if it were feeding the core.

Behaviour by state

StateWhat changes
ActiveEverything shown. Gravity tilt, depth parallax and seconds each have an editor switch.
Power saving, or battery at 20 percent or lessAccelerometer, parallax, seconds and slosh stop. The surface sits level relative to the watch.
Battery under 20 percent with the battery fluidThe fluid turns ember-red in every theme.
Always-onBlack screen. Core outline, outline numerals, a waterline hairline, twelve ring dots, the hour cursor and the date. No fill, pods, weather or sensors.

Build plan

  1. Foundation

    New name and app ID so it installs beside the mechanical face. Editor settings and slots. One geometry file with glyph paths and layout constants, shared by the asset generator, the browser preview and the tests. Numeral PNGs rasterized from it.

  2. Core spike, on the watch first

    Glass, masked fluid, battery level, waterline numerals with the blend-mode inversion and refraction offset, and accelerometer tilt. This carries the most risk, so it is installed and checked on the Watch6 Classic before anything is built on top of it.

  3. Chronocast ring

    Hour sectors, temperature terrain, condition colours and textures, day/night band, labels, UV marks, cursor, and the no-data fallbacks. Confirm how many forecast hours Samsung's firmware provides.

  4. Pods, conduits, date, weather line, event bar

    Every complication type, the unavailable states, goal-lit conduits, and non-overlapping editor selection areas.

  5. Bubbles, seconds spark, depth parallax

    Bubble count and its rise animation, the rim spark with its tail, and Gyro offsets for the glass highlight and pods.

  6. Slosh

    Rename the companion's tap provider to Core tap, shorten its window, allow the new face ID, bump the companion versions, then add the damped wave to the face.

  7. Always-on, low battery, themes

    The outline scene, the power and battery gates, the ember-red warning fluid, and the four fluid colours.

  8. Tests, previews, docs

    Expression tests for level, tilt, forecast mapping across midnight and 12/24 hours, gating, slot order and selection bounds. Picker preview rendered from the browser preview. README and device checklist, then a final run on the watch.

Checked on the watch

  • Masks and blend modes inside a rotating group: correctness and frame cost.
  • Accelerometer update rate, jitter and battery cost. If it costs too much, gravity tilt becomes opt-in.
  • Forecast depth: how many hourly entries the firmware supplies.
  • Always-on offload with outline numerals.
  • Legibility exactly at the waterline, at low levels, in sunlight.

Defaults to confirm

  • Name and ID: Meniscus, com.bobbfwed.emberphase.meniscus, installed alongside the mechanical face.
  • Default fluid: Battery, in Plasma.
  • Pod defaults: steps, active minutes, active calories, heart rate.
  • Small text in IBM Plex Mono (open font licence), bundled with the face.
