Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: watch-face design approval board; concept artwork, not an installed watch face.
Primary request: Invent a futuristic round Wear OS watch face called PARALLAX, with a distinct asymmetric visual identity and three user configurable complication docks. Produce an exceptionally polished, restrained industrial interface design, fully face-on and flat enough to judge legibility. Landscape presentation board around 1536x1024. Black / near-black background. One very large circular active watch face on the left, one smaller circular ambient face on the upper right, one smaller circular alternate-complications face on the lower right. No physical watch hardware, no wrist, no perspective.
Main face design: a pure-black circular canvas. Huge warm-ivory narrow geometric numerals: hour "10" above and left of center, minute "08" below and right of center. Keep numerals fully readable. Across the space between them, a delicate folded translucent cyan ribbon extends on an ascending diagonal from lower left to upper right: two offset angular wireframe surfaces with a pinched hinge and sparse fine contour lines, like an architectural pleat seen in cross-section. This is the DAY FOLD, a visual of local civil-day progress; one bright cyan seam marks now and a short caption "DAY 42%" sits near the bottom. Make the fold a uniquely sculptural diagram, not a generic waveform, concentric rings, or a large decorative illustration. Thin, precise, static-looking vector geometry with restrained internal shading. Top date "SAT 07 NOV". Slim interrupted battery rail along the left inside edge, small "73%" label. Three equal compact open-corner trapezoidal complication docks in the lower third with generous dark separation, using sample data "6,240" / "STEPS", "36" / "MIN", "410" / "KCAL". Tiny goal bars where appropriate. All text legible. Discreet "PARALLAX" near the base.
Ambient miniature: same offset hour and minute positions, warm-ivory thin outlined numerals, small date and 73% battery, black background, fold and complication cards absent. Alternate miniature: same design but complication docks show "18°" / "WEATHER", "14:30" / "NEXT", "68" / "BPM". Values are illustrative.
Palette: pure OLED black, warm ivory #EDF2EA, ion cyan #77F4E6, acid lime #C9F279 only for an active datum, small muted gray labels. Do not flood the face with glow. Crisp typography; data docks and date must have practical hierarchy. A few quiet editorial labels on the board: "PARALLAX", "A folded view of the day.", "ACTIVE", "AMBIENT", "YOUR DATA".
Avoid: analog hands, gears, mechanical objects, moon imagery, calendar drums, circular fitness rings, gradients across the whole face, starfields, scanlines, excessive HUD clutter, fake medical predictions, logos, watermarks. Do not use any existing images as references.
